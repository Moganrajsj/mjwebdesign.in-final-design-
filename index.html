Frequently asked questions

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp.

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on the number of pages and the features you need. Tell us about your business and we’ll send a clear quote before we start.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most business websites take a few weeks, depending on the size of the site and how quickly we receive your content.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your business name, logo, photos and the details you want on the site. If you don’t have these yet, we can help you create them.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every site we build is designed for phones first and tested on phones, tablets and computers.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. We build e-commerce websites where customers can browse your products and order online.

Do you help with hosting, domain and updates?

Yes. We can set up your domain and hosting, and we stay on after launch to fix, update and improve your site.

Can you design my logo and brand?

Yes. We create your logo, colors and fonts so your website, social posts and print pieces all match.
