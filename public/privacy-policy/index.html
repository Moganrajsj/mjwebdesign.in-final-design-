1. Who we are

MJ Web Designs is a website design and digital services studio based in Dharmapuri, Tamil Nadu, India. In this policy, “MJ Web Designs”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the team operating this website at www.mjwebdesign.in.

This policy applies to information collected through this website and its project enquiry form. It does not automatically apply to third-party websites you may visit through links on our site.

2. Information we collect

When you submit our project enquiry form, we may receive the information you choose to provide:

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your email address, if you choose to provide it.
  • The service you are interested in and the project details you enter in the message field.

We may also receive basic technical information when your browser accesses the website, such as an IP address, browser and device information, pages requested and request timestamps. This information may be recorded by the website hosting or infrastructure providers for delivery, security and troubleshooting.

Please do not include passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information in the enquiry form.

3. How we use your information

We use enquiry information for reasonable business purposes, including to:

  • Respond to your questions and project enquiries.
  • Understand the services and requirements you have described.
  • Contact you by phone, email or WhatsApp where appropriate to discuss your enquiry.
  • Prepare estimates, proposals and follow-up communication you request.
  • Maintain, secure and improve the operation of our website.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and handle disputes or misuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the enquiry details submitted through this form to send unrelated promotional messages without an appropriate basis or your permission.

4. Sharing and service providers

We may share the minimum information needed with service providers that help us operate the website or respond to enquiries. Based on the current website setup, these may include:

  • Vercel: website hosting and delivery infrastructure.
  • Google / Gmail: email infrastructure used to deliver project enquiries to our business inbox.
  • Google Fonts: font delivery for the website's visual presentation.

These providers may process technical or submitted information as necessary to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or to address security and fraud concerns.

5. Cookies and technical data

Our website is primarily an informational website. We do not currently use the enquiry form to collect payment details or require visitors to create an account. Hosting and infrastructure providers may use logs or similar technical mechanisms to deliver, protect and troubleshoot the website.

If we add analytics, advertising pixels, embedded content or other technologies that materially change how information is collected, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking some technologies may affect how parts of a website work.

6. Storage, retention and security

Enquiry submissions are transmitted to our website endpoint and, when delivery succeeds, sent to our business email inbox. We keep enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a potential or active business relationship, maintain appropriate records and meet legal requirements.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. However, no website, email transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You can choose not to submit the enquiry form. If you have already sent us information, you can contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate details, or request deletion where we are able to do so and are not required to retain it.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some information may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes or protect legitimate business interests. Your rights may also depend on the laws that apply to your circumstances.

8. Children's privacy

This website and our services are intended for businesses and general audiences, not specifically for children. Please do not submit a child's personal information through the enquiry form. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Please review this page periodically.
