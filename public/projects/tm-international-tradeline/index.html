TM International Tradeline — Spices & Fresh Produce Exporter

A business website that introduces an Indian export company, organises its product catalogue and gives international buyers a clear route to make trade enquiries.

Discuss a similar projectVisit TM Tradeline website

Business categorySpices and fresh produce export
Project typeBusiness website

Project overview

TM International Tradeline presents itself as an exporter of Indian spices and fresh produce, based in Dharmapuri, Tamil Nadu. The website gives the business a digital catalogue for buyers to explore product groups and a contact path for wholesale and export enquiries.

Business context

International buyers need to understand the supplier, the available product categories and how to begin a conversation about specifications, quantities and destination requirements. The website brings those details together in one place.

Website experience

The live site combines a company introduction, product catalogue, sourcing and quality information, export-related knowledge articles, frequently asked questions and a detailed enquiry form.

What the website presents

The catalogue and content are organised to help trade visitors move from discovering the company to understanding its product range and making an enquiry.

01

Fresh produce

A product group for fresh vegetables and farm-sourced produce, with examples such as tomato, raw red chilli and semi-husked coconut.

02

Whole spices

A catalogue group for whole spice products, including cardamom and cashew nut.

03

Spice powders

A product category for ground spices such as turmeric, coriander, red chilli, cumin, pepper, garlic and ginger powders.

04

B2B enquiry paths

Calls to enquire about a product category, individual products and a detailed buyer enquiry form.

05

Company and sourcing information

Sections describing the business, its Dharmapuri base, direct sourcing approach and quality-control messaging.

06

Trade knowledge centre

Articles and buyer information covering spice selection, product handling, export considerations and logistics topics.

Who this website serves

The website is structured for business visitors who need product and supplier information before starting a trade conversation.

  • Wholesale buyers exploring Indian spices and fresh produce.
  • Food processors and commercial buyers comparing product categories.
  • Importers looking for supplier information and a direct enquiry route.
