AYLI — Women’s fashion and online retail

An online fashion storefront created to give a women’s clothing brand a dedicated place to present collections and help shoppers explore products.

Business categoryWomen’s fashion and online retail

Project overview

AYLI is presented as a women’s fashion label. Its online shop groups products into categories such as dresses, western tops, office wear, kurta sets, kurtis, bottoms and accessories, giving shoppers several ways to browse the catalogue.

Business context

Fashion shoppers often want to browse by garment type and compare product details before deciding what to buy. A dedicated online store gives the brand a structured catalogue and a central destination for its digital presence.

Project details

The currently accessible ayli.in storefront displays categories including dresses, western tops, budget deals, office wear, accessories, kurta sets, bottoms, kurtis, dupattas and bags. The page also exposes product search and cart elements.
