Project overview

The live website presents Sriram Broadband Services under the Airlink Broadband brand. It introduces home and business connectivity, explains the provider's offering and gives visitors routes to explore plans, check coverage and contact the team.

Business context

Broadband customers often compare available speeds, monthly plans, coverage and support before they enquire. The website needs to make these details easier to find while giving residential and business visitors a clear path to the next step.

Project details

On the live website, visitors can find sections for plans, infrastructure, speed testing, coverage, shop, about, careers and contact. The site identifies Dharmapuri and Chennai as service locations and uses Airlink Broadband as its public-facing brand.
