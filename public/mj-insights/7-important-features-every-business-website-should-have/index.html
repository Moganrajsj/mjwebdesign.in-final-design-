7 Important Features Every Business Website Should Have

A business website should help visitors understand what you do and make the next step obvious. These seven essentials create a useful foundation for most small and medium-sized business websites.

By MJ WEB DESIGNS · Published 8 November 2026

1. A mobile-friendly layout

Many visitors will open your website on a phone. Text should be readable, buttons easy to tap and layouts should work without horizontal scrolling.

2. Clear navigation

Use simple menu labels and a logical page structure. Visitors should be able to find your services, products, company information and contact details without guessing.

3. Specific service or product information

Explain what you offer, who it is for and how customers can get started. Dedicated pages are often clearer than placing every detail on one crowded page.

4. Visible contact options

Show the contact methods your business actually uses—phone, email, WhatsApp, enquiry form or booking. Test forms regularly and explain what happens after submission.

5. Genuine trust signals

Use accurate business information, real project examples, authentic testimonials with permission, relevant certifications and clear policies. Never publish invented reviews or unsupported claims.

6. Good performance and security

Optimise image sizes, avoid unnecessary scripts, use HTTPS and keep the website platform and dependencies maintained. A fast, secure experience helps visitors use the site comfortably.

7. Search-friendly foundations

Give each important page a unique title and description, use one clear main heading, write descriptive links, add useful image alt text and make sure important pages can be crawled and included in your sitemap.

Key takeaway

A strong business website is not defined by how many effects it has. It is defined by how clearly it communicates, how well it works on phones and how easily visitors can take the next step.
