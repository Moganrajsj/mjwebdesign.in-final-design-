AI Image Generation vs AI Video Generation: What's the Difference?

AI image generation creates still visuals from prompts or reference images. AI video generation creates moving sequences, often from text, images or existing clips. Both can support creative work, but they solve different communication problems.

By MJ WEB DESIGNS · Published 8 November 2026

AI image generation

Image models can create illustrations, product concepts, campaign visuals, backgrounds and edits to existing images. A still image is useful when the message can be communicated in one frame, such as a poster, thumbnail or product visual.

AI video generation

Video models generate sequences with motion and may support camera movement, scene changes, sound or speech depending on the system. They are useful for concept films, short social clips, storyboards and visual experiments.

Why video is harder

A video model must maintain subject identity, object shape, lighting, scene continuity and plausible motion across many frames. It may introduce flicker, inconsistent details or unexpected movement. Longer clips can be more difficult to control.

Choosing the right format

Choose an image for a clear single message, product hero or graphic. Choose video when movement, sequence or atmosphere adds meaning. A campaign may use both: generate a visual direction first, then animate selected elements.

Production workflow

Start with a clear brief, audience, aspect ratio and visual references. Review generated assets for factual accuracy, brand consistency, unwanted text, rights and artefacts. For video, inspect every shot and frame transitions before publishing.

Responsible use

Check each tool's terms for commercial use, likeness, copyright and data handling. Do not imply that synthetic scenes or people are real. Label or disclose AI-generated content when required by platform rules or applicable law.
