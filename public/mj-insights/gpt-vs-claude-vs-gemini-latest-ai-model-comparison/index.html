GPT vs Claude vs Gemini: Latest AI Model Comparison (November 2026)

GPT, Claude and Gemini are three major AI model families, but there is no universal winner. Their strengths change by model version, task, product interface and tool access. This guide is a practical comparison snapshot for 8 November 2026—not a permanent ranking.

By MJ WEB DESIGNS · Published 8 November 2026

The model families to know

OpenAI's current GPT-6 family includes specialist variants such as GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol. Anthropic's current Claude 5.5 family includes Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5. Google's Gemini 4 family includes Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon. These names refer to specific model releases; app defaults and API availability can differ.

Reasoning and research

For difficult multi-step tasks, compare the reasoning mode, available tools, response quality and reliability—not just the model name. OpenAI describes GPT-6 Astra as targeting complex professional, science and software tasks. Anthropic positions Opus 5.5 for agentic coding and knowledge work. Google positions Gemini 4 Argon for complex real-world work across coding, finance, legal and science.

Coding and software development

For coding, test each model on your own repository and workflow. Ask it to understand an existing codebase, make a small change, run tests, explain failures and avoid unrelated edits. Agentic coding performance depends on the model plus its terminal, file, browser and test tools.

Long documents and multimodal work

Check the exact model's context limit and supported inputs before choosing it. Some variants accept images, audio, video or very long text; other variants may have different limits. A large advertised context window does not guarantee perfect recall of every detail.

How to choose

Choose by repeatable tests: accuracy on your tasks, latency, price, context needs, privacy, tool integrations and how often you need to correct the output. For sensitive or high-impact decisions, keep human review in the loop.

The takeaway

Try the same representative prompts across the current models and score them against your real requirements. Model releases move quickly, so revisit this comparison when providers publish new versions.
