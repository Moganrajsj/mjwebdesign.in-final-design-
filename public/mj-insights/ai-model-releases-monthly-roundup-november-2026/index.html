AI Model Releases: November 2026 Roundup

AI releases arrive quickly, and announcement dates, product availability and API access are not always the same. This roundup records selected official announcements available as of 8 November 2026. It is not a complete list of every model released this month.

By MJ WEB DESIGNS · Published 8 November 2026

OpenAI: GPT-6 family updates

OpenAI's model release notes include GPT-6 family updates and a 30 October 2026 announcement for GPT-6 Rosalind Research, a specialised research offering for approved life-sciences customers. Availability is limited and should not be confused with general ChatGPT access. See the official release notes for eligibility and access details.

Anthropic: Claude 5.5 family

Anthropic announced Claude Opus 5.5 on 22 September and Claude Sonnet 5.5 on 28 September 2026. The company describes Opus as focused on agentic coding and knowledge work, and Sonnet as a faster, lower-cost complement. Check current product and API availability before selecting a model.

Google: Gemini 4 Argon

Google announced Gemini 4 Argon on 13 October 2026, describing it as a model for complex tasks across coding, finance, legal, cybersecurity and science. Google says it is available through its developer and enterprise platforms, with access in consumer products depending on subscription.

Meta: Muse Image and Muse Video

Meta introduced Muse Image and previewed Muse Video in July 2026. The announcement describes image generation and editing capabilities, along with a video model preview featuring native audio. Availability varies by product and region.

How to follow releases responsibly

Use official release notes for launch dates and availability. Distinguish previews from generally available models, and provider-reported benchmark claims from independently reproduced results. Check model IDs and deprecation notices before updating production integrations.
