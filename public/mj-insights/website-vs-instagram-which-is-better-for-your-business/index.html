Website vs Instagram: Which Is Better for Your Business?

A website and an Instagram account do different jobs. Instagram can help people discover your brand and follow updates. A website gives customers a structured place to explore your business, compare options and take action.

By MJ WEB DESIGNS · Published 8 November 2026

Instagram helps you reach and engage an audience

Instagram is useful for visual updates, short videos, launches, offers and conversations. It can help your business stay visible to followers and participate in relevant communities.

A website organises the information customers need

Your website can explain services in detail, show product categories, publish policies, answer common questions and provide a consistent contact route. You can organise information in a way that suits your business.

Search visibility is different

Public social posts may appear in some search results, but a website gives you dedicated pages that can be crawled and indexed by search engines. Each page can focus on a specific service, product or helpful topic.

Control and customer journey

On Instagram, the platform controls the interface and many parts of the experience. On your website, you have more control over navigation, page content, forms and the journey from first visit to enquiry.

Use both channels together

Share useful content on Instagram and direct interested people to the relevant page on your website. Add your website link to your profile, keep contact details consistent and use campaign-specific landing pages when needed.

Which should you choose first?

If customers mainly need to see frequent visual updates, social media can be a useful starting point. If they need detailed information, product catalogues, quotations or bookings, a website becomes especially important. Many businesses should plan for both.

Key takeaway

It is not really website versus Instagram. Use Instagram to attract and engage people, and use your website as the dependable place where they can understand your offer and contact or buy from you.
