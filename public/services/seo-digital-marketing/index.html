Frequently asked questions

Can SEO guarantee first-page rankings?

No one can responsibly guarantee a particular Google ranking. SEO work improves site structure, relevance and crawlability, while search performance depends on many factors outside a provider's control.

Do you work on a website built by another developer?

We can review an existing website and discuss SEO improvements based on its platform, access and current technical setup.

How soon will SEO results appear?

Timing varies by website, competition, content and search engine processing. Search Console helps monitor crawling, indexing and performance over time.

Can SEO and paid campaigns work together?

They serve different roles. SEO supports organic discovery, while paid campaigns can help promote specific offers or services. The plan depends on your goals and budget.
