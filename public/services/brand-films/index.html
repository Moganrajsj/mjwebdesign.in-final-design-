Frequently asked questions

Can a video be created for social media?

Yes. We can plan the concept and format around the social platform and placement you have in mind.

Can you create a product video?

We can develop product-focused video content based on the product information, footage or visual assets available for the project.

Do I need a script?

A script is not required at the start. We can help structure the message and sequence as part of the agreed brief.

What affects the cost of a brand film?

Duration, concept, production needs, footage, editing complexity and delivery formats all affect the scope and estimate.
