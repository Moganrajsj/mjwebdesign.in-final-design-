Brand Identity & Logo Design

A brand identity gives your business a recognisable visual language. We help bring the key pieces together so your logo, colours, typography and everyday marketing materials feel like they belong to the same brand.

Who this is useful for

A good project starts with a clear business need. This service may be a fit when you want to:

  • New businesses preparing to introduce their brand to customers.
  • Existing businesses whose visual materials look inconsistent.
  • Brands planning a website or marketing refresh and needing a coherent visual direction.
